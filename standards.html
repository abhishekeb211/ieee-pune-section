IEEE Standards Association

IEEE Blockchain & Distributed Ledger Standards

Published and developing IEEE standards for blockchain systems, distributed ledger technologies, smart contracts, digital assets, identity, interoperability, and sector-specific applications.

IEEE Blockchain & Distributed Ledgers Standards Committee — C/BDLSC

Founded 5 September 2019, the IEEE Blockchain and Distributed Ledgers Standards Committee (C/BDLSC) oversees all IEEE blockchain standards development. The committee manages published standards, active working groups, and new project proposals.

Chair: Ming Li (li.ming@ieee.org) • IEEE Staff: Michael D. Kipness (m.kipness@ieee.org)
