Local Identity

IEEE Pune Section & Regional Ecosystem

Established on June 26, 2010, the IEEE Pune Section belongs to IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific), serving the vibrant Pune metropolitan area. Pune is a premier academic and technological epicentre, hosting over 100+ engineering colleges, major IT services companies, and FinTech centers.

The Blockchain Group operates at the intersection of Pune's research institutes and software industry. By focusing on distributed consensus protocols, smart contract analysis, and decentralized identity systems, we connect local student talent and professional members with global research standards and enterprise DLT networks.

Section Milestones & Node

  • June 26, 2010: Official establishment of the IEEE Pune Section.
