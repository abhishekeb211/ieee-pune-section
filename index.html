Message from the Section Chair

"The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. Here in Pune Section, we take immense pride in representing IEEE's core mission by empowering engineers, faculty, entrepreneurs, and students across one of India's most dynamic technological epicenters."

"With over 65 student branches, numerous specialized technical chapters, and passionate affinity groups, IEEE Pune Section serves as a vibrant platform for research excellence, standardization, and societal impact. We collaborate closely with premier academia, industry R&D labs, and startups to shape a brighter, more sustainable future."

Join us in our mission — together, we inspire the next generation of innovators, researchers, and technology leaders.
