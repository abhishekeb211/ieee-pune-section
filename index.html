About IEEE Blockchain Pune Section

The IEEE Pune Blockchain Group operates under the IEEE Blockchain Technical Community (BCTC) within IEEE Region 10. It connects the IEEE Pune Section, IEEE Computer Society Pune Chapter, and leading Pune institutions — PCCOE, VIIT, MMCOE — to advance blockchain research, standards, education, and professional collaboration across Maharashtra.

Pune Blockchain Ecosystem

IEEE Blockchain Pune Section & Regional Infrastructure

The IEEE Pune Section belongs to IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific), serving the vibrant Pune metropolitan area with 2,500+ members, 24+ Student Branches, and 15+ technical societies/chapters. Pune is a premier academic and technological epicentre hosting 100+ engineering colleges, major IT services companies, and FinTech centers.

The IEEE Pune Blockchain Group — chaired by Dr. Sonali D. Patil (also Region 10 Coordinator for IEEE Blockchain Groups) — operates at the intersection of Pune's research institutes and software industry. Activities span blockchain research, industry outreach, faculty development, student programs, smart contracts, cybersecurity, sustainability, and local institutional collaborations with PCCOE, VIIT, and MMCOE.

Standard for Data Format for Blockchain Systems
