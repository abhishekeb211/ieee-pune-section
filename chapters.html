Ecosystem & Chapters

Communities & Interest Groups

Special Interest Groups (SIGs), student branches, and technical societies collaborating on blockchain innovation in Pune.

Blockchain Special Interest Group (SIG)

Specialized research and developer cohort focusing on consensus protocols, smart contract auditing, and zero-knowledge cryptography led by Mrs. Rohini Pise.

Key Focus: Hands-on Solidity labs, smart contract security audits, honors program

IEEE Computer Society Pune Chapter

The flagship computing society chapter in Pune coordinating joint symposiums, conferences, and faculty development programs in decentralized software engineering.

Key Focus: NSBC 2026 Symposium, AI-Blockchain integration, research publications

65+ Student Branches (SAC)

Student branch network across Pune engineering institutions driving BlockHack hackathon teams, DApp developer bootcamps, and peer tutorials.

Key Focus: BlockHack 2026, campus Web3 clubs, student leadership congress

Women in Engineering (WIE) Blockchain

Empowering women researchers and engineers in Web3, cryptography, and distributed systems through dedicated mentorship and symposium tracks.

Key Focus: WIE tech talks, research fellowships, coding bootcamps

Young Professionals (YP) in Web3

Early-career engineers and startup founders working on enterprise distributed ledgers, smart contract deployment, and decentralized finance infrastructure.

Key Focus: Startup mentorship, industry roundtable meets, networking sessions

Center of Excellence (CoE) Partnerships

Collaborative testbed infrastructure with Pune's premier institutions and tech industry partners for deploying distributed consensus validation nodes.

Key Focus: Enterprise testnets, pilot DApp testing, formal verification

IEEE Blockchain Pune Section

Official technical chapter and specialized activity group under the IEEE Pune Section (Region 10), dedicated to advancing research in distributed ledger systems, smart contract auditing, and Web3 technologies.
