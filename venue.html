Travel & Transit Guidance

✈️

By Air

Pune International Airport (PNQ) serves the region with frequent domestic and international flights. Pre-paid cabs, app-based ride-sharing (Ola, Uber), and auto-rickshaws are easily accessible for direct transit to conference venues.

🚂

By Train

Pune Junction (Major Terminus): Connected to all major Indian cities. Local suburban trains also run regularly between Pune Junction and Lonavala, seamlessly connecting the entire metro region.

🚗

By Road

Pune is exceptionally well-connected via the 6-lane Mumbai-Pune Expressway and national highways. Excellent state bus transport (MSRTC) and local city bus networks (PMPML) link all venues with central transit hubs.
