Venues & Travel Information

Host venues for IEEE Blockchain conferences, symposiums, and institutional laboratories across Pune, Maharashtra, with air, rail, and road travel guidance.

Travel & Transit Guidance

PNQ

By Air — Pune International Airport

  • Pune International Airport (PNQ), Lohegaon
  • Direct links to Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata
  • International hub connections including Dubai
  • Pre-paid taxis and app rides (Uber, Ola) to conference venues
PUNE

By Train — Pune Junction

  • Pune Junction (PUNE) — major Indian Railways terminus
  • Express / superfast services including Vande Bharat, Shatabdi, Duronto
  • Suburban links to Shivajinagar, Pimpri, Chinchwad, Akurdi (near PCCOE), Lonavala
Expressway

By Road

  • 6-lane Mumbai–Pune Expressway (~3 hours from Mumbai International Airport / BOM)
  • NH 48 and NH 65 corridors
  • Intercity luxury buses, MSRTC Shivneri, private cabs round the clock
  • Local: Pune Metro lines and PMPML bus routes
