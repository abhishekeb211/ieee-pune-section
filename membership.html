Membership Categories & Access

Category Annual Cost / Benefit What You Get
IEEE Student Member Highly Discounted Rates Full access to all workshops, Solidity labs, BlockHack hackathons, plus IEEE publications and student travel grants.
IEEE Professional Member Standard IEEE Rates Leadership opportunities, publishing rights, IEEE Senior Member pathways, and invitations to technical roundtable panels.
Non-IEEE Member (Affiliated) Free Community Access Access to select public workshops, open industry guest lectures, and local Web3 developer networking meets.

What Membership Covers

  • Hands-on Solidity, Rust & DApp developer bootcamps
  • Opportunities to work on local Web3 development projects
  • Certificates signed by IEEE Pune Section leaders
  • Access to global technical publications and research papers
  • Discounted entry to NSBC 2026 and international symposia
