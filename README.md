# IEEE Blockchain Pune Section Web Portal

The official web portal and research hub for **IEEE Blockchain Pune Section** (IEEE Region 10 &bull; Asia-Pacific).

---

## 🌐 Pages & Structure

- **Home (`index.html`)**: Flagship homepage with interactive hero slider, pillars, section milestones, featured activities, important dates timeline, metric counters, and institutional sponsor strip.
- **About (`about.html`)**: Chapter background, IEEE Region 10 node context, relationship with global IEEE, and mission & vision.
- **Committee (`committee.html`)**: Full organizing committee with Faculty Advisors, Student Branch Officers, and Technical Blockchain Leads.
- **Focus Areas / Tracks (`tracks.html`)**: Six core research domains — Blockchain Systems, Smart Contracts & Verification, Security & Privacy, Distributed Systems & DLT, FinTech & Digital Assets, AI & Domain Applications.
- **Events (`events.html`)**: BlockHack 2026 hackathon, NSBC 2026 symposium, Solidity & Hardhat workshops, and important dates.
- **Speakers (`speakers.html`)**: Keynote speakers, panelists, workshop leads, and topic listings.
- **Membership (`membership.html`)**: Student, Professional, and Affiliated tiers, bootcamp access, benefits table, and registration links.
- **Venue & Travel (`venue.html`)**: Host institutions in Pune, transit by Air (PNQ), Train (Pune Junction), and Road.
- **Contact (`contact.html`)**: Chapter coordinates, Coordinator Dr. Santwana Gudadhe, and interactive inquiry form.
- **Communities & SIGs (`chapters.html`)**: Blockchain Special Interest Groups, Computer Society Chapter, WIE, YP, and 65+ Student Branches.
- **Education & Labs (`education.html`)**: Applied Solidity/Hardhat developer bootcamps and Faculty Development Programs.
