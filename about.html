About IEEE Blockchain Pune Section

The IEEE Pune Blockchain Group under the IEEE Blockchain Technical Community (BCTC) and IEEE Pune Section (Region 10), advancing blockchain research, standards, and education across Maharashtra.

Organizational Identity & Scope

IEEE Blockchain Pune Section represents the collective blockchain initiatives operating under the IEEE Pune Blockchain Group — a recognized local community within the global IEEE Blockchain Technical Community (BCTC) in IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific).

Chaired by Dr. Sonali D. Patil (who also serves as the Region 10 Coordinator for IEEE Blockchain Groups), the group bridges IEEE's global blockchain standardisation efforts, regional conferences (ICBDS, ICDLT, ICCUBEA), and leading academic engineering institutions across Pune and Maharashtra.

IEEE Pune Section and Regional Context

Established on June 26, 2010, the IEEE Pune Section belongs to IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific) and serves as a premier catalyst for technical advancement across the Pune metropolitan area and Maharashtra. Pune Section encompasses:

  • 2,500+ Active Members spanning academia, research labs, and technology enterprises
  • 24+ Student Branches across premier autonomous and affiliated colleges
  • 15+ Technical Society Chapters & Affinity Groups including Computer Society, Communications Society, and WIE
  • A region hosting 100+ engineering institutions and major IT, automotive, cybersecurity, and FinTech centers

Core Activities & Infrastructure

01

Flagship Conferences & Publications

Annual IEEE conferences including ICBDS (International Conference on Blockchain and Distributed Systems Security) and ICDLT (International Conference on Distributed Ledger Technologies) with proceedings indexed in IEEE Xplore and Scopus.

02

Standards Development & IEEE SA Collaboration

Engagement with the IEEE Blockchain & Distributed Ledgers Standards Committee (C/BDLSC) on published standards (IEEE 2418.2, 2418.7) and 30+ active standards projects (P3201–P3230).

03

Institutional Labs & Centers of Excellence

Collaborations with regional academic institutions including PCCOE, VIIT, and MMCOE's dedicated Blockchain Technology Lab for applied research and prototyping.

04

Pan-India Network & Student Ecosystem

Active connection with 10 India-based IEEE Blockchain Groups and student communities at premier IITs (Bombay, Delhi, Kharagpur, Madras, Roorkee).

Mission and Objectives

01

Scholarly & Technical Leadership

Foster high-impact research, peer-reviewed publications, and technical discourse in decentralized systems, smart contracts, cryptographic protocols, and blockchain security.

02

Workforce & Student Capability

Equip students and practitioners with industry-aligned skills through Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), hands-on smart contract laboratories, and hackathons.

03

Trusted Ecosystem Integration

Promote the adoption of open, interoperable, and standards-compliant blockchain technologies across governance, finance, supply chains, healthcare, and energy sectors.
