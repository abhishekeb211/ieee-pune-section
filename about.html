About the Chapter

IEEE Blockchain Pune Section is the official technical chapter and specialized activity group under the IEEE Pune Section, promoting blockchain research, cryptographic protocol design, and developer ecosystems across Pune and the wider Maharashtra region.

We support student and professional capability-building, technical exchange, research publications, and hands-on workshops in cryptography, smart contract auditing, and decentralized architectures.

IEEE Pune Section and Region 10 Affiliation

Established on June 26, 2010, the IEEE Pune Section belongs to IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific) and serves as a premier catalyst for technical growth in the Pune metropolitan area. Pune is a major technology hub with over 100+ engineering institutions and a massive IT, cybersecurity, and fintech services ecosystem.

The Blockchain Group was established to leverage Pune’s dense academic and industry network, promoting structured research and engineering practice in blockchain, consensus algorithms, cryptographic protocols, and smart contract verification.

Relationship with Global IEEE

By organizing hands-on workshops, technical symposiums, and local developer meetups, IEEE Blockchain Pune Section connects local engineering students and professionals with the global IEEE Blockchain Technical Community, the IEEE Standards Association, and the IEEE Computer Society.
