Research & Technical Tracks

Technology Focus Areas

Key research themes and technical domains driving the activities, symposium tracks, and publications of the IEEE Blockchain Pune Section.

Blockchain Systems

Core blockchain architecture, consensus mechanisms, layer-2 scalability, cross-chain interoperability, and throughput benchmarking.

Key Focus: Consensus protocols, sharding, cross-chain systems, Rollups & bridges

Smart Contracts and Verification

Design, auditing, automated vulnerability scanning, formal analysis of EVM bytecode, and developer tooling for smart contract execution.

Key Focus: Contract security, language tooling, formal methods, static analysis

Security and Privacy

Cryptographic models, threat intelligence, privacy-preserving zero-knowledge protocols (zk-SNARKs/STARKs), key management, and compliance.

Key Focus: Zero-knowledge systems, key management, privacy protocols, post-quantum crypto

Distributed Systems and DLT

Distributed architectural principles, Byzantine fault tolerance, state machine replication, and enterprise permissioned ledgers (Hyperledger, Corda).

Key Focus: Fault tolerance, replication, permissioned ledgers, decentralized storage

FinTech and Digital Assets

Blockchain applications in institutional finance, Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) architectures, tokenization of assets, and automated settlement.

Key Focus: CBDC infrastructure, settlement, compliance automation, DeFi primitives

AI and Domain Applications

Integration of decentralized AI with blockchain, verifiable model weights, federated workflows, and sector-specific deployment in supply chains and healthcare.

Key Focus: Federated workflows, audit trails, provenance, AI agents on-chain

Call for Papers & Symposium Submissions

Submissions addressing original, unpublished research across any of the 6 focus areas are welcomed for presentation at the National Symposium on Blockchain & Cyber Security (NSBC 2026) and ICBDS Conference series. Accepted and presented papers will be considered for inclusion in the IEEE research proceedings.
