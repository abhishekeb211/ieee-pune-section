Workshops & Training

Blockchain Education & Labs

Applied technical training, Solidity & Rust smart contract masterclasses, and Faculty Development Programs in Pune.

Solidity & Hardhat Labs

Comprehensive hands-on training on smart contract writing, unit testing with Mocha/Chai, local testnet deployment, and gas optimization techniques.

Key Training: Hardhat, Remix, Foundry, OpenZeppelin standard contracts

Smart Contract Security & Auditing

Specialized masterclasses on detecting reentrancy bugs, integer overflows, access control flaws, and running static analysis tools like Slither and Mythril.

Key Training: Slither, Mythril, Formal Verification, Audit Checklist

Faculty Development Program (FDP)

Empowering engineering professors and researchers in Pune with structured curriculum design in blockchain technology, consensus protocols, and cryptography.

Key Training: Academic syllabus integration, lab setup, IEEE research papers

IEEE Blockchain Pune Section

Official technical chapter and specialized activity group under the IEEE Pune Section (Region 10), dedicated to advancing research in distributed ledger systems, smart contract auditing, and Web3 technologies.
